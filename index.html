Executive Summary

VERDICT: CONDITIONAL

Worth pursuing as a sourcing and derivative play. Not worth pursuing as a standalone bulk trade or a new nitration plant.

  • Small molecule, small pool. Supplied rows show India buying 37.5 MT from China in 2025 (to 31-Oct) worth $199,118. Even with a healthy trading margin that is a five-figure dollar profit pool.
  • Published market sizes fail a basic test. Vendor figures run from US$120 M to US$30.84 billion. At $5.31/kg those imply 22,600 MT to 5.8 million MT a year, against 37.5 MT seen for India. Treat them as unusable.
  • Two names run the India trade. Smilax Laboratories took 77% and Zhejiang Top Hankook supplied 84% of 2025 bulk volume. A newcomer has to win one of very few accounts.
  • The price gap is real but guarded. Chinese material lands near $5.75/kg before creditable IGST. Indian-made material sells at about $17 to 20/kg. Japan and reagent brands pay the premium; price-led Indian buyers import direct.
  • The window is supply risk. China's 2026 nitration automation rules, the sulphur shock and the sulphuric acid export ban all hit this chemistry. The supplied rows stop at October 2025, so none of this shows up in the price data yet.
  • Value sits one step downstream. The same Chinese trader lists 3-nitrophthalic anhydride at about five times the acid price, and Indian generics now sell pomalidomide in the US, with apremilast generics due in the US from 2028.

Product Overview & Identity

What it is

3-Nitrophthalic acid (3-NPA) is an aromatic diacid with a nitro group next to one carboxyl. That crowded substitution pattern is why drug makers buy it: reduction gives the 3-amino series used for the phthalimide core of pomalidomide and apremilast, and half-esterification followed by Curtius rearrangement gives the 2-amino-3-nitrobenzoate used in candesartan and azilsartan [2][3][4]. It is also the classic start for luminol [5].

Synonyms: 3-NPA, 3-nitro-1,2-benzenedicarboxylic acid, m-nitrophthalic acid, o-nitrophthalic acid (a confusing vendor synonym), NSC 3120, Pomalidomide Impurity 8, Apremilast Impurity 86 [6].

Why yield is so low. Nitration splits roughly 50/50 between 3- and 4-isomers [21]. The 3-isomer is filtered off because the 4-isomer is far more soluble in water. Older processes discarded the 4-isomer as waste; CN1740138A recovers it from mother liquor [25]. Economics hinge on selling that co-product, listed at higher prices than the acid itself [26]. A 1925 method claiming 85 to 90% gave only 20% when one chemist repeated it, and that thread repeats the published warning about violent nitration [27].
